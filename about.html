About StarRupture.net

We're an independent, community-driven guide site dedicated to helping players master StarRupture. No official affiliation—just passionate players creating practical, version-verified resources for the community.

🎯 Our Mission

StarRupture launched into Early Access on January 6, 2026, and players immediately started asking the same questions: "How do I survive the first night?", "Where do I find water?", "Why does my game crash?"

We created StarRupture.net to provide fast, accurate, no-fluff answers to these questions. Our goal is simple: help you find what you need in 30 seconds or less, so you can get back to playing.

What we believe

  • Speed matters — Guides should answer your question immediately, not after 500 words of introduction
  • Accuracy over quantity — We'd rather have 10 correct pages than 100 outdated ones
  • Version awareness — Early Access games change fast; we track and update when they do
  • Community-first — Player feedback directly shapes our content priorities

💎 Our Editorial Standards

Every guide on StarRupture.net follows these principles to maintain quality and trustworthiness:

✅

Version Verified

Each guide displays the game version it was tested on. When major patches drop, we review and update affected content.

🔍

In-Game Tested

We don't copy-paste from other sources. Our guides are based on actual gameplay testing and verification.

📝

Correction-Friendly

Found something wrong? We encourage players to report errors. We fix confirmed issues quickly.

No Fluff Policy

We skip the filler. No "As a player, you might wonder..." introductions. Just the information you need.
