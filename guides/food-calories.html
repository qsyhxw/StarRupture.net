StarRupture Food & Calories Guide

Complete food guide with all consumables, stats, and crafting tips. Learn what to eat early game, how to unlock better foods, and how to manage Toxicity.

Quick Answer

Early Game: Eat Polifruit (+11 Calories) from orange shrubs.
Mid Game: Craft Calorie Gel (+65 Calories, no Toxicity).
Late Game: Use Nutri Gel (+40 Cal, +50 Hydration) for efficiency.

📈 Food Progression

Early Game (First 30 min)

  1. Eat Ration Packs from landing pod (no Toxicity)
  2. Forage Polifruit (+11 Cal) as you explore
  3. Keep Hydrobulbs for hydration
  4. Bank surplus food at base

Mid Game (Recipe Station unlocked)

  1. Research Calorie Gel (60 Polifruit + 25 Grubblers)
  2. Set up Food Station production
  3. Switch from raw foods to crafted consumables
  4. Keep Purfins for Toxicity emergencies

Late Game (Extended runs)

  1. Use Nutri Gel for inventory efficiency
  2. Stack Calorie Block Capsules for long exploration
  3. Bring Clear Capsules for passive Toxicity decay

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Don't wait until empty: Eat when Calories drop below 30% to avoid panic foraging
  • Balance, don't spam: High-Toxicity foods are fine occasionally, not every meal
  • Bank food at base: Always have a food reserve for respawns and emergencies
  • Inventory efficiency: Nutri Gel replaces carrying separate food and water
  • Plan for long trips: Calorie Block Capsules + Aqua Block Capsules = 45+ min worry-free

❓ FAQ

What is the best early game food?

Polifruit (+11 Calories) is everywhere and has no Toxicity. Ration Packs from your landing pod are even better but limited.

How do I unlock Calorie Gel?

Research it at the Recipe Station. Common requirements: 60 Polifruit + 25 Grubblers. Check your in-game UI as costs may vary by patch.

What food gives the most Calories without Toxicity?

Calorie Gel (+65 Cal) and Calorie Bar (+50 Cal) have no Toxicity. For balanced stats, Nutri Gel (+40 Cal, +50 Hydration) is excellent.

How do I reduce Toxicity?

Purfins (-70 Toxicity) are the strongest cleanser. Antidote Shards give +60 HP and -40 Toxicity. Both remove positive buffs, so time them carefully.

Is it better to eat raw or crafted foods?

Early game, raw foods are fine. Mid to late game, crafted foods are significantly more efficient (Calorie Gel = ~6x a Polifruit per slot).

My recipe costs don't match this guide. Why?

StarRupture is in Early Access. Recipe costs may change between patches. Always check your in-game Recipe Station for current requirements.
