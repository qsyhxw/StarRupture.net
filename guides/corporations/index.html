🏢 Corporation Progression

StarRupture Corporation Guide

Complete guide to corporation unlock order, level rewards, and optimal progression path. Learn which corporations to prioritize for efficient gameplay.

Recommended Unlock Order

Prioritize these corporation levels for optimal early-game progression:

1

Moon Energy Corporation → Level 3

Unlock: Map - Essential for navigation and finding resources safely. Top priority.

2

Selenian Corporation → Level 2

Unlock: Fabricator - Critical for processing materials needed by other corporations.

3

Future Health Solutions → Level 6

Unlock: Food Station - Prevents hunger/thirst issues for long-term survival.

4

Griffits Blue Corporation → Level 2

Unlock: UPP-7 Pistol - Basic weapon for defense once survival is stable.

5

Clever Robotics → As Needed

Storage & Automation - Upgrade as your base expands and needs more logistics.

💡 Strategy Tip: Bring all corporations to Level 2 first for baseline features (low cost), then focus on the priority order above.

How to Level Up Corporations

Two methods to increase corporation levels:

Method 1: Data Points

  • Earned from the Analyzing Station
  • Good for early levels (low cost)
  • Save for unlocking new recipes later

Method 2: Orbital Cargo Launcher (Recommended)

  • Export materials specific to each corporation
  • Most sustainable long-term method
  • Requires efficient production lines

Corporation Details

Moon Energy Corporation

Focus: Power Generation & Mapping

Key Unlocks

  • Level 1: Basic Solar Panel, Small Battery, Power Pole
  • Level 3: Map (Essential)
  • Level 5: Medium Solar Panel
  • Level 8: Advanced Solar Panel

Selenian Corporation

Focus: Materials, Fabrication & Mining

Key Unlocks

  • Level 1: Base Core, Ore Excavator, Smelter, Habitat
  • Level 2: Fabricator (Essential)
  • Level 6: Helium-3 Extractor
  • Level 10: Assembler
  • Level 11: Greater Lifegiver LEM

Future Health Solutions

Focus: Survival, Healing & Food

Key Unlocks

  • Level 1: Habitats, Ladders, Airlocks
  • Level 2: Regeneration Chamber
  • Level 4: LEM Station
  • Level 6: Food Station (Essential)
  • Level 11: Greater Lifegiver LEM

Griffits Blue Corporation

Focus: Weapons, Defense & Combat

Key Unlocks

  • Level 1: Basic Platforms, Advanced Platforms
  • Level 2: UPP-7 Pistol (Essential)
  • Level 3: Grenade
  • Level 5: War Bonds x60
  • Level 6: Defense Turret
  • Level 11: Greater Lifegiver LEM, Greater Shieldgiver LEM

Clever Robotics

Focus: Logistics, Drones & Automation

Key Unlocks

  • Level 1: Corporate Terminal, Orbital Cargo Launcher, Rail v.1
  • Level 3: Storage Depot v.1
  • Level 4: Building Drone
  • Level 6: Rail v.2
  • Level 11: Rail v.3
