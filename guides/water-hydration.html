StarRupture Water & Hydration Guide

Complete hydration guide with all drinks, stats, and crafting tips. Learn what to drink early game, how to unlock better fluids, and manage dehydration in hot biomes.

⚡ Quick Answer

Early Game: Drink Hydrobulbs (+15 Hydration) from blue glowing plants.
Mid Game: Craft Aqua Chew (+100 Hydration, no Toxicity).
Late Game: Use Nutri Gel (+40 Cal, +50 Hydration) for efficiency.

Hot biomes drain Hydration faster. These items help.

Hot Biomes: Desert and volcanic areas drain Hydration 2-3x faster. Prepare extra Aqua items or use Block Capsules before entering.

📈 Hydration Progression

Early Game (First 30 min)

  1. Use Ration Packs from landing pod (no Toxicity)
  2. Forage Hydrobulbs (+15) near water sources
  3. Collect Pricklers for combined Cal/Hydration
  4. Establish Hydrobulb farming routes

Mid Game (Recipe Station unlocked)

  1. Research Aqua Chew for +100 Hydration
  2. Set up Food Station production
  3. Switch from raw to crafted hydration items
  4. Carry Nutri items for space efficiency

Late Game (Extended runs)

  1. Use Nutri Gel exclusively (+50 Hyd, +40 Cal)
  2. Bring Aqua Block Capsules for desert exploration
  3. Stack Termo Res Plasm for volcanic biomes

⚖️ Hydration vs Calories Priority

Situation Priority Reasoning
Hot/Desert biome Hydration Depletes 2-3x faster
Cold/Night Calories Hydration stable, stamina matters
Combat heavy Equal Both deplete from sprinting/dodging
Base building Calories Low movement, steady drain

💡 Tips & Best Practices

  • Check biome before traveling: Hot = extra Aqua items
  • Hydrobulb farming: They respawn near water — loop back regularly
  • Nutri Gel late game: One slot covers both needs
  • Block Capsules: 45 min worry-free for serious exploration
  • Don't panic drink: Hydration has clear warning phases

❓ FAQ

What is the best early game hydration source?

Hydrobulbs (+15 Hydration) are everywhere near water and have no Toxicity. Look for glowing blue plants.

How do I stop dehydrating so fast in the desert?

Use Aqua Block Capsules (stops hydration loss 45 min) or Termo Res Plasm (doubles heat resistance). Both add Toxicity, so plan detox.

What drink gives the most Hydration without Toxicity?

Aqua Chew (+100 Hydration) is the best pure hydration item with no Toxicity.

Should I carry separate food and water, or use Nutri items?

Early game, separate is fine. Mid to late game, Nutri Gel (+50 Hyd, +40 Cal) is much more inventory efficient.

Do Pricklers give both Calories and Hydration?

Yes! Pricklers from purple cacti are great early game finds that restore both stats.

How do Aqua Block Capsules work?

They completely stop Hydration loss for 45 minutes. You still need to manage Calories. Good for desert runs, but +45 Toxicity is significant.
