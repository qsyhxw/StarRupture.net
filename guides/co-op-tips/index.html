👥 Multiplayer Guide

StarRupture Co-op Guide

Complete guide to 4-player co-op: team roles, division of labor, priority unlocks, and multiplayer strategies. Maximize efficiency with coordinated teamwork.

Co-op Basics

StarRupture supports up to 4 players in host-based multiplayer. The host creates a persistent world where all progress is saved.

💡 Multiplayer Setup: Host loads save → Pause menu → Change visibility to "Friends Only" or "Invite Only" → Press "Go Online"

Team Roles

While StarRupture has no formal classes, specializing into these 4 roles maximizes team efficiency:

🛡️ Defender / Soldier

  • Patrol base perimeter during Rupture cycles
  • Clear hostile wildlife and enemy camps
  • Escort Explorers into high-risk areas
  • Manage turret ammunition and defenses

🔧 Builder / Engineer

  • Design and expand base infrastructure
  • Construct production lines and rail systems
  • Optimize factory layouts for efficiency
  • Manage power distribution

🔬 Researcher / Scientist

  • Manage OCL contracts and corporation progression
  • Automate production lines for materials
  • Research and unlock new technologies
  • Coordinate resource allocation

🗺️ Explorer / Biologist

  • Map the open world and scout new areas
  • Locate rare resources and ore deposits
  • Mark points of interest for the team
  • Gather samples and research materials
💡 Tip: Enemy waves scale with player count. Coordinate defenses accordingly.

Priority Unlocks for Co-op

Focus on these corporation levels for coordinated team play:

1. Moon Energy Level 3 - Map

  • Full map reveal for coordinated exploration
  • Essential for long-term planning

2. Selenian Level 2 - Fabricator

  • Process materials for delivery items
  • Advance all corporations efficiently

3. Griffits Blue Level 2 - Pistol Blueprint

  • Ensure all players have basic weapons
  • Critical for early defense

Co-op Strategies

Communication

Use clear callouts for effective coordination:

  • "Low on copper" - Resource shortages
  • "Swarm east side" - Enemy locations
  • "Heat wave incoming" - Environmental hazards

Resource Management

  • Coordinate resource gathering to avoid duplication
  • Automate production lines early
  • Set up smelters near excavators
  • Establish efficient rail transportation

Base Building

  • Choose locations with basic and advanced resources
  • Minimize enemy presence near base
  • Build essential structures: Base Core, Habitat, Airlock, Solar Panels
  • Leave space for expansion

Important Caveats

⚠️ Avoid "Deconstruct All": Using the "Deconstruct All" feature in co-op can cause desync issues. Manually deconstruct structures instead.
💡 Host Management: The host should coordinate major base changes. Non-hosts may experience lag when placing many structures at once.
  • Save frequently - co-op sessions can desync
  • If players see different base states, restart the session
  • Enemy wave difficulty scales with player count
  • Only the host's world persists - guests don't keep progress in their own saves
