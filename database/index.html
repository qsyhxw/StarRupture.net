Core Resources Overview

The four essential resources you'll need throughout your Arcadia-7 journey.

Titanium

Gray rocky veins, appears as gray circles on map. Found north/northeast of Lander. Used for Basic Building Material and Titanium Bars.

Extraction: Ore Excavator | Processing: Smelter → Fabricator

Wolfram

Copper-brown veins with small archway. Common near Lander. Essential for Wolfram Wire and corporation progression.

Extraction: Ore Excavator | Processing: Smelter → Fabricator

Calcium

Pale rock areas, white rocks with black spots. Often near water. Required for Calcium Blocks, Calcite Sheets, and advanced recipes.

Extraction: Ore Excavator | Unlock: Recipe Station

Helium-3

Purple geysers/vents. Mid-game resource east of Lander. Critical for Accumulators, Aerogel, and glass production.

Extraction: Helium-3 Extractor (Slenian Lv6)

Corporation Unlocks

Six mega-corporations provide technology in exchange for resources.

Selenian Corporation

Core infrastructure: Base Core, Basic Item Printer, Ore Excavator, Smelter. Fabricator at Level 2, Assembler at Level 10.

Moon Energy

Power systems: Solar Generator v1 and Analyzing Station at Level 1. Recipe Station at Level 2. Map unlock at Level 3.

Griffits Blue

Military tech: UPP-7 Pistol at Level 2, Grenades at Level 3, Equipment Upgrade Station at Level 4, Defense structures at higher levels.
