What are Blueprints?

Advanced crafting recipes found in abandoned structures across Arcadia-7.

Blueprint Basics

Blueprints unlock advanced crafting recipes that cannot be obtained through the Recipe Station alone. They are found in blue-glowing loot containers inside abandoned bases and ruins.

  • Location: Abandoned bases (SMBs), ruins, underground caves
  • Identification: Blue-glowing containers (blueprints) vs Red containers (supplies)
  • Unlock: Once found, blueprints permanently unlock recipes at Recipe Station
  • Map Access: Griffits Blue Lv3 unlocks map markers for locations

Preparation Tips

  • Combat Ready: Bring ~300 bullets minimum for hostile encounters
  • Lighting: Some locations are dark - bring flashlight or headlamp
  • Storage: Clear inventory space for loot and blueprints
  • Timing: Scout during daytime for better visibility

Purple Haze - Selenian Mining Base

The most important early-game blueprint location with Rotor and Tube blueprints.

Purple Haze (SMB)

Selenian Mining Base
Direction

Landmark

Blueprints
Rotor + Tube

The first major abandoned base most players encounter. Located northwest of the landing pod, identifiable by a distinctive broken antenna dish on the roof. Contains two critical blueprints essential for mid-game progression.

Blueprints Found Here

⚙️
Rotor Blueprint
Used for: Solar Generators, advanced machinery
🔧
Tube Blueprint
Used for: Helium-3 Extractors, piping systems

How to Access

  • Entry Point: Jump onto roof via nearby rocks, enter through broken wall/roof opening
  • Structure: Two-story building with multiple rooms
  • Blue Chests: Contain blueprints - prioritize these
  • Red Chests: Contain supplies and resources

Combat Warning

Vermin enemies spawn inside and around the building. Bring approximately 300 bullets and be prepared for close-quarters combat. Clear enemies before looting.

All Known Blueprints

Complete list of blueprints and their crafting uses.

Fabricator Blueprints

  • Rotor: Ti Rods + Wolfram Wires → Used in Solar Generators, machinery
  • Tube: Ti Sheets + Ti Rods → Used in Helium-3 Extractors, piping
  • Stabilizer: Rotors + Ti Rods → Used in advanced structures
  • Stator: Ti Housing + Wolfram Wires → Used in power systems

Furnace Blueprints

  • Inductor: Wolfram Wires + Components → Used in electronics
  • Advanced Glass: Calcium + Helium-3 variants → Used in optics

Blueprint Priority Order

Recommended order for acquiring blueprints based on progression:

  • 1. Rotor + Tube: Purple Haze SMB - Essential for power infrastructure
  • 2. Stabilizer: Enables advanced building construction
  • 3. Stator: Required for late-game power systems
  • 4. Inductor: Electronics and advanced components
