💎 6 Resources Documented

Resource Locations

Complete guide to finding and extracting all resources on Arcadia-7. Locations, visual identification, extraction methods, and unlock requirements.

Jump to: Titanium Wolfram Calcium Helium-3 Sulphur Quartz

Titanium Ore

Early Game
Appearance
Gray rocky veins
Map Marker
Gray circles
Extractor
Ore Excavator
Processing
Smelter → Bars

The most common early-game resource. Gray rocky deposits with muted coloring. Essential for Basic Building Material, Titanium Bars, Sheets, and Beams. Found abundantly near the starting area.

Primary Locations

  • North of Lander: Large clusters of veins
  • Northeast of Lander: Major deposits extending from start
  • Ground rocks: Small gray rocks scattered everywhere
  • Meteorites: Can be harvested from fallen meteorites

Extraction Methods

Manual: Harvester Laser on veins or pick up loose rocks
Automated: Place Ore Excavator on flat deposits, connect to Smelter via rails

Wolfram Ore

Early Game
Appearance
Copper-brown veins
Map Marker
Orange circles
Extractor
Ore Excavator
Processing
Smelter → Bars

Reddish-brown ore with distinctive gate-like structure on deposits. Essential for Wolfram Bars, Plates, Wire, and Powder. Often the first resource you'll encounter after leaving the Lander.

Primary Locations

  • Left of Lander: Initial deposit immediately visible
  • North/Northeast: Large clusters of veins
  • Far North: Significant vein further on map
  • Ground rocks: Reddish small rocks scattered

Extraction Methods

Manual: Harvester Laser on veins, pick up loose chunks
Automated: Ore Excavator on flat deposits → Smelter → Furnace (for Powder)

Calcium Ore

Early-Mid Game
Appearance
White/pale plateaus
Map Marker
Sandy/beige patches
Extractor
Ore Excavator
Processing
Smelter → Blocks

Large pale white plateaus often containing water puddles. Critical progression gate for Calcium Blocks, Calcite Sheets, and Glass production. White orbs scattered on ground also contain calcium.

Primary Locations

  • North of Lander: Large sandy plateau deposits
  • Northeast: Significant veins with water pools
  • Near water sources: Often found near ponds
  • Meteorites: Up to 30 Calcium + Meteorite Hearts

Extraction Methods

Manual: Harvest 3-5 units from small white rocks
Automated: Ore Excavator → Smelter (unlock Calcium Block recipe first: 1,000 Ca Ore)

Helium-3

Mid Game
Appearance
Purple geysers/vents
Map Marker
Purple indicators
Extractor
Helium-3 Extractor
Unlock
Selenian Lv6

Distinctive purple geysers emitting purple smoke. Critical for Glass, Accumulators, Aerogel, and advanced power systems. Requires specialized extractor unlocked mid-game.

Primary Locations

  • East of Lander: Near Grubbler trees at mountain base (2 vents)
  • Far Northeast: Triple geyser cluster past large Calcium vein
  • Far North: Snow/winter biomes past Calcium plateaus
  • Note: One geyser blocked by abandoned base (unusable)

Extraction Methods

Requires: Helium-3 Extractor (Selenian Lv6)
Setup: Deploy Base Core near geyser → Place extractor → Connect power (high electricity demand)

Selenian Corporation Level 6 required

Late Game
Appearance
Yellow with toxic gas

Bright yellow areas
Extractor
Sulphur Extractor
Unlock
Selenian Lv9

Hazardous yellow deposits surrounded by corrosive gas clouds. Required for advanced late-game crafting. Extraction only possible immediately after Rupture events when fumes temporarily dissipate.

Primary Locations

  • East of Lander: Beyond Calcium deposit, near cliffs
  • Far East: Large deposit near Helium-3 areas
  • Warning: Corrosive environment damages player

Extraction Methods

Requires: Sulphur Extractor (Selenian Lv9)
Timing: Place extractor immediately after Rupture when gas clears temporarily

Selenian Corporation Level 9 required

Quartz

Late Game
Appearance
Clear/white crystals
Map Marker
Underground caves
Extractor
Manual harvest
Unlock
Cave access

Crystalline mineral found in underground cave systems. Required for advanced electronics and late-game components. Cave entrances become accessible after Rupture events reveal new terrain.

Primary Locations

  • Underground caves: Crystal formations on cave walls
  • Post-Rupture areas: New cave entrances after seismic events
  • Deep exploration: Requires lighting and preparation

Extraction Methods

Manual: Harvester Laser on crystal formations
Note: Cave exploration requires adequate lighting and combat readiness

Resource Gathering Tips

  • Early Game: Focus on Titanium and Wolfram near the Lander
  • Meteorites: Harvest fallen meteorites for bonus resources + Meteorite Hearts
  • Automation: Set up Ore Excavators early to build passive income
  • Map Markers: Use the map (Moon Energy Lv3) to locate resource deposits
  • Processing Chain: Ore → Smelter → Fabricator for most materials
