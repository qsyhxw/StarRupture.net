How Weapons Work in StarRupture

Weapons are purchased with War Bonds at the Equipment Upgrade Station (unlocked at Griffits Blue Lv4). You can carry 2 weapons at a time. Dying with non-pistol weapons causes them to be lost and must be re-crafted.

  • War Bonds are earned from Griffits Blue contracts and looting
  • Modifications require War Bonds to unlock, then Basic Building Materials to craft
  • Ammo is crafted at the Basic Item Printer

Your first ranged weapon in StarRupture. The UPP-7 is automatically equipped when unlocked and serves well in early game. However, it has no available modifications, causing its effectiveness to diminish as you progress. Consider upgrading to the MAR-9 Phantom as soon as possible.
