What are LEMs?

Logistics Enhancement Modules are permanent character upgrades.

LEM Basics

LEMs (Logistics Enhancement Modules) are powerful upgrades that enhance your character's abilities. Once acquired, they provide permanent bonuses.

  • Acquisition: Found in abandoned bases, loot containers, and enemy drops
  • Slots: Limited number of LEM slots available
  • Stacking: Some LEMs stack, others have diminishing returns
  • Categories: Red (Combat), Blue (Movement), Green (Survival)

LEM Tiers

LEMs come in different power levels:

  • Basic: Standard effect (e.g., "Lifegiver")
  • Greater: Enhanced effect (e.g., "Greater Lifegiver")
  • Superior: Maximum effect (rare, late-game)

Where to Find LEMs

Primary locations for acquiring Logistics Enhancement Modules.

Abandoned Bases

The primary source for LEMs. Explore ruins and abandoned structures.

  • Purple Haze: Selenian Mining Base - Multiple LEMs + Blueprints
  • SMB Locations: Scattered across the map, marked on radar
  • Underground: Cave systems contain rare LEMs

Enemy Drops

Hostile creatures occasionally drop LEMs on death.

  • Elite Enemies: Higher chance for Greater-tier LEMs
  • Rupture Spawns: Enemies during Rupture events
  • Boss Creatures: Guaranteed LEM drops

Loot Containers

Blue-glowing containers found throughout the world.

  • Standard Crates: Basic LEMs
  • Rare Containers: Greater-tier LEMs
  • Hidden Stashes: Check behind structures and in corners

LEM Optimization Tips

  • Priority: Greater Lifegiver > Greater Scalper > Endurancegiver
  • Stacking: Multiple same-type LEMs stack additively
  • Balance: Mix survival and combat LEMs for versatility
  • Exploration: Thoroughly search abandoned bases for hidden LEMs
  • Rupture Events: Farm enemies during Ruptures for LEM drops
