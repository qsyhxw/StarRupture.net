Recipe Unlock Guide

How to unlock crafting stations and recipes in StarRupture.

Recipe Station

The central hub for unlocking most recipes. Required for technological progression.

  • Unlock: Moon Energy Corporation Level 2
  • Method: Submit Wolfram Bars via Orbital Cargo Launcher, or 200 Data Points
  • Function: Donate materials/Data Points to unlock new recipes

Blueprints

Advanced recipes require finding Blueprints in the world.

  • Locations: Abandoned bases, station ruins, underground caves
  • Appearance: Blue-glowing loot containers
  • Examples: Rotor, Tube, Stabilizer, Stator blueprints
  • Notable: "Purple Haze" (Selenian Mining Base) contains Rotor + Tube

* Data Point Variance

Some unlock costs (like Wolfram Plate: 200-400 DP) show discrepancies between sources. Costs may vary by game version. Check Recipe Station in-game for current requirements.
