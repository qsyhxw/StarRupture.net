Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best weapons in StarRupture?

The MAR-9 Phantom assault rifle is the best all-rounder. Grenades are extremely powerful for crowd control. The SLAMS-12 shotgun excels at close range. See full tier list →

Where is the best base location?

Early game: near your Lander for safety. Mid-to-late game: the central map area with access to Titanium, Wolfram, Calcium, and Helium-3 deposits. See location rankings →

How do I survive the Rupture event?

When you see "Wave Warning," you have about 20 seconds to get inside a sealed building (Lander or Habitat with Airlock). Always carry materials for an emergency "pocket base." Survival guide →

What are the production ratios?

A common early-game ratio: 1 Miner → 1 Smelter → 1 Fabricator → 3 Orbital Launchers. Full ratios guide →

Can I host a dedicated server?

Yes. StarRupture includes an experimental dedicated server tool on Steam. You can also use third-party hosts like BisectHosting or GTXGaming. Server setup guide →

Is this site official?

No. StarRupture.net is an independent fan site. We verify guides by game version and update when things change. Not affiliated with Creepy Jar.
